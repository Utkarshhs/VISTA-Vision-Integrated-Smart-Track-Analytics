About VISTA

Intelligent Predictive Maintenance for Railway Infrastructure — built to modernize how Indian Railways monitors, inspects, and maintains critical track components.

Our Vision

VISTA (Visual Intelligence for Structural Track Assessment) is a centralized web dashboard designed to simulate and demonstrate how AI-powered predictive maintenance can transform railway infrastructure management.

The platform addresses a critical gap: traditional railway inspections are manual, reactive, and prone to human error. VISTA introduces a data-driven, AI-augmented workflow that predicts component failures before they happen — reducing derailment risk, minimizing downtime, and optimizing resource allocation.

Core USP: Offline-First Resilience

Railway field engineers often operate in remote areas with zero or unreliable connectivity. VISTA is designed with an offline-first architecture that ensures full inspection and data capture capability without an internet connection. Data syncs automatically when connectivity is restored.

  • Full inspection workflow available offline
  • Local data storage with automatic cloud sync
  • AI analysis queued and processed upon reconnection
  • No data loss during connectivity gaps

Hierarchical User Roles

VISTA implements a three-tier role hierarchy mirroring Indian Railways' operational structure, ensuring the right information reaches the right personnel.

Regional Controller

Oversees the entire sector's risk map. Views aggregated CII scores across all hubs, dispatches maintenance alerts, and monitors sector-wide metrics and trends.

Hub Supervisor

Manages engineer assignments at the hub level. Prioritizes inspection tasks, assigns field engineers to high-risk components, and tracks active assignments.

Field Engineer

On-ground inspector who performs physical inspections, uploads photos for AI analysis, and can override AI assessments via the Truth Check calibration system.

Data & AI Architecture

VISTA employs a dual-AI approach combining mathematical prediction with visual verification:

  • CII (Component Integrity Index): A 0-100 score calculated from stress factors — age, load (GMT), rainfall index, and thermal gradient. Scores below 30 trigger critical alerts.
  • Gemini AI Vision: Photo-based damage verification that can override mathematical CII predictions when visual evidence of critical damage is detected.
  • Temporal Delta Analysis: Tracks rate of decay between inspection cycles to detect acceleration in component degradation.
  • Truth Check (Human-in-the-Loop): Field engineers can disagree with AI assessments, logging calibration events for model improvement.

Deployment Strategy

VISTA follows a phased deployment approach. The current phase (Phase 1) delivers a centralized web dashboard simulation that demonstrates the full workflow — from risk monitoring to AI-assisted inspection to human verification.

  • Phase 1 (Current): Centralized Web Dashboard — simulation of the complete predictive maintenance workflow
  • Phase 2 (Planned): Native Field App — offline-capable mobile application for field engineers
  • Phase 3 (Planned): Production Deployment — integration with live railway data feeds and real sensor networks
